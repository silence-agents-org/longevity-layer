# Research

**[SIGNALS: latest global signals by region](SIGNALS.md)** · [Daily digests](daily/README.md)


Questions only. No product claims, no medical claims.

1. **Agreement across devices.** When a person switches instruments, how should two series be compared before joining them? Classic method-comparison work plots differences against means instead of using correlation.
   Source: Bland JM, Altman DG. "Statistical methods for assessing agreement between two methods of clinical measurement." *The Lancet*, 1986. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(86)90837-8 (accessed 2026-10-26)
2. **Step vs slope.** What threshold separates a source-change step from real gradual change in noisy self-reports, and how sensitive is the 2-SD rule used here?
   Source: Page ES. "Continuous inspection schemes." *Biometrika*, 1954 (CUSUM change detection). https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/41.1-2.100 (accessed 2026-10-26)
3. **Long-horizon comparability.** Population indicators of years lived in good health are defined with fixed methods across decades. Which self-reported daily signals stay comparable over 5+ years when wording, scales and habits change?
   Source: World Health Organization, "Healthy life expectancy (HALE) at birth (years)", indicator metadata. https://www.who.int/data/gho/indicator-metadata-registry/imr-details/66 (accessed 2026-10-26)
4. **Logging-method artifacts.** How large is the apparent change when a person moves from manual entry to automatic capture, and should that boundary always be treated as a new segment? (Open, no source yet; contributions welcome.)
